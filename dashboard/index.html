        How ChronoTrace works
        
      
        
        
        1 · Load & cleanRCAEval window (5 min before / after). Drop duplicate time columns, mesh artefacts and IP host columns; fold datastores into their owner (carts-db → carts, ts-auth-mongo → ts-auth-service).
        
        
        →
        
        
        2a · MetricsRobust z vs healthy median/IQR, 3-point median smoothing, upper-half sustained drift × KS shift × novelty: share of faulty points above the highest level the series already reached while healthy.
        
        
        2b · LogsVectorised templating with PII masking. Local error bursts boosted when novel or carrying a stack frame; "call to X failed" errors discounted (they blame the callee). New-template bursts net of normal churn.
        
        
        2c · TracesServer exclusive time (own work, children subtracted). Network gap per call edge; a service is blamed in proportion to how many and what share of its edges slowed.
        
        
        →
        
        
        3 · Gate & fuseEach modality normalised, then weighted by its decisiveness (top-1 vs top-2 margin) in this incident. Complete traces count double. No per-dataset weights.
        
        
        →
        
        
        4 · Evidence bundleRanking, what/where/why, rule-based next steps, input and evidence SHA-256.
        
      
        
      
        DETERMINISM FIREWALL — nothing below can change anything above
        
      
        
        
        5 · Explanation layerGemma 4 (Gemini API) or a local Ollama model writes a schema-locked JSON runbook from the bundle only. Every service, metric and command is checked against the evidence; any mismatch falls back to a deterministic template.
        
      
        
      
        
        
        Why not just ask an LLM?
        Raw telemetry for one incident is hundreds of thousands of log lines and spans. Benchmarks show LLM agents fed raw telemetry rank poorly, and their answers cannot be reproduced. Here the model only verbalises a finished, hashed result.
        
        
        Why not BARO?
        BARO scores a metric by its single largest post-fault z-score. Bursty series (e.g. Redis disk flushes) and zero-IQR counters win that contest in most RE2/RE3 cases. The novelty filter and the trace/log scorers fix exactly those failure modes — see the ablations on the Benchmark tab.
